# The 4-Week Plan

The offer is confirmed, the script is written. What is left is how to spread,
and that is arithmetic. Your work, in one line: **ask hours a day once, read the lane off the
founder's own files, and write `squad/90-day-plan.md`, one page: a header and 4 rows, every row
the same cycle, sized to those hours.** The founder's part is one number, one yes, the hour of
the block, and one line every Sunday.

**The cycle is 3 beats, and only 3. Run what already wins. Measure the results. Improve what
won.** Run is Monday to Saturday, the making usually Monday and Tuesday, the send or publish from
Wednesday. Measure and Improve are Sunday's, about 10 minutes.

This skill runs in ANY founder's repo. Read `.claude/squad-roots.md` first. Two rows are this
skill's own: `hours a day`, written at beat 1, and `daily lane` (OUTREACH, CONTENT or ADS),
written at the yes. `ads gate` is a1's row, read here with the other two. **Never write into the
`lane` row.** That row is the Winning Scrape's and holds a content-research path.

## The run map (where you run, where you STOP)

| Beat | Mode |
|---|---|
| 1 THE HOURS | HUMAN INPUT: one number, and nothing prints before it |
| 2 THE LANE AND THE PLAN | AUTO: the files read, the lane, the arithmetic, the plan written to the template; the lane line, the path, the block line and the 4 Run cells printed |
| 3 THE YES | **STOP · GATE: the founder's yes and the block hour; a different lane word or hours number rewrites.** Then AUTO: the stamp, the roots row, and the line that says what to type next |
| 4 THE SUNDAY CELL | HUMAN INPUT: "Put this in week N's Measure: ____ / Improve: ____". Then AUTO: the 2 cells written, the row printed, the line that says what to type next, and nothing else |
| 5 RENEW | AUTO: the 4 Measure and Improve cells, `squad/pipeline.md` and the lane's own log read. Then HUMAN INPUT: the hours again, with the lane read printed. Then AUTO: plan 2 written whole under its own heading, 4 new rows. Then **STOP · GATE: the yes and the block hour**, then the stamp, the roots rows, and beat 3's line back |

The beat numbers are the section numbers below. Beats 1 to 3 write plan 1 and run once. Beat 4
is the one thing written between the yes and the renewal. Beat 5 is `renew`, at week 4's Sunday
or any day after it, and it ends on beat 3's gate. One question, one print, one gate,
in each mode. Never pause an automated beat to ask a small question; never run through a gate
because the answer seems obvious.

**Resuming.** The rule keys on the OUTPUTS, never on what a session remembers: check them in this
order and continue at the first one missing or incomplete.

| Missing or incomplete | Resume at |
|---|---|
| `.claude/squad-roots.md` has no `hours a day` row | beat 1 |
| the row is there and `squad/90-day-plan.md` does not exist | beat 2 |
| the plan's newest block has a header line with no `confirmed <date>` stamp | the path, the block line and the 4 Run cells printed again, then the gate only, never a rewrite: beat 3's gate when the unconfirmed block is the first in the file; beat 5's gate when it is not |
| the newest block is confirmed and the message says "put this in week N" | beat 4, on that block's row N |
| the newest block is confirmed and today is before week 4's Sunday | on a bare `/the-90-day-plan` or a "continue", one line: which week this is, which Measure and Improve cells are still blank, the 2 forms the Improve cell takes, and the sentence that fills them, `Put this in week N's Measure: ____ / Improve: ____`; on the word `renew`, one line naming week 4's Sunday, the day `renew` opens, and nothing is written |
| the newest block is confirmed and today is week 4's Sunday or past it | on a bare `/the-90-day-plan` or a "continue", say so in one line and offer `/the-90-day-plan renew`; on the word `renew`, beat 5, a new block. Never rewrite a confirmed block |

## The outputs

1. `squad/90-day-plan.md`: THE file, one page per plan, written to the exact shape in
   `references/plan-template.md`. Beat 4 writes 2 cells of it. `renew` appends a block under it
   and never edits a line above.
2. The `hours a day` and `daily lane` rows in `.claude/squad-roots.md`.

Nothing else gets written.

## 1 · THE HOURS

**First, at every entry, a self-check, silent when it passes.** Two files must open.
`references/plan-template.md`, inside THIS skill's folder next to `SKILL.md`: missing, stop and
tell the founder to finish the install, the whole skill folder, `references/` included.
`squad/business.md`: missing, stop on one line, g4 or g5 builds it. Either stop comes before the
question, asks nothing and writes nothing.

**Then the one question, when the roots file holds no `hours a day` row.** Nothing prints before
the question, not the lane, not a file read, not a table:

> Hours a day for the system, beyond the hour you spend on people?

One number. Write it into `.claude/squad-roots.md` as `| hours a day | N |`. A wrong number
gets corrected at the gate, never by a second question. The hour on people is not in the number
and is never asked about: it is a habit, and this plan does not count it.

## 2 · THE LANE AND THE PLAN

**Read, in this order, asking nothing about any of it.**

| File | What it answers |
|---|---|
| `.claude/squad-roots.md` | `hours a day`, and the `ads gate` row (a1) |
| `squad/business.md` | who buys: a buyer you can list by name, or one you cannot; WHO in its own words, THE PROMISE, and THE NUMBERS on a cold document |
| `squad/mep/*/plan.md`, and `deck.html` beside it | built for whom, and their town (THE BUYER line); the deck is there only when `deck.html` is |
| `squad/clients/*/notes.md` | who asked for a named thing; THE NEXT STEP, dated or not; the town, where a note names one |
| `squad/ads-money-card.md` | the daily number (a2), when the ADS read holds |

**The lane is read, never asked.**

| The files say | The lane | Chris's level rule |
|---|---|---|
| the buyer in `business.md` can be listed by name (an owner, a realtor, a clinic, a job title in a city), and there is something to show: the deck in `squad/mep/` (its `deck.html`), or a client folder with a dated next step | OUTREACH | proof in hand, outreach |
| the buyer cannot be listed by name (a consumer, a creator, a broad audience), or there is nothing to show yet | CONTENT | nothing yet, content |
| the `ads gate` row reads open (a1): money that can leave every month without touching rent | ADS | the money is there, ads |

With the `ads gate` row open, ADS is the read, above the 2 rows; its numbers come off
`squad/ads-money-card.md` (a2). With the row shut or not on disk, the 2 rows decide.

**The count is one line of arithmetic.** The hours are the system's, whole.

| Lane | The arithmetic |
|---|---|
| all | minutes a day for the system = hours a day x 60 |
| OUTREACH | by hand a day = minutes / 3, the minutes on the touches by the founder's hand until the routine sends for them (o4). A week, Wednesday to Saturday, = a day x 4 |
| CONTENT | the minutes, and 1 recording a week: recorded Tuesday, published Wednesday, the same slot every week |
| ADS | the daily number off `squad/ads-money-card.md` (a2), all 7 days, x 7 for the week, behind a1's gate. No card, the spend row reads `your daily number (a2)` and no figure is printed. The minutes go on the creative, then on the read and the leads |

The plan carries this arithmetic as a small table. Never print a weekly number the send days do
not produce.

**What goes in each column.**

| Column | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Week | the number and its dates, Monday to Sunday |
| Run | written now, from the founder's own files, never from the template's example: the asset made that week AND the send or publish that puts it in front of strangers. The files go in first: what THE PROMISE says the founder builds, in the product word, opens week 1's Run; every next step the files already hold by name (THE NUMBERS' notes on a cold document, THE NEXT STEP in each client folder) goes in, in the file's own words, in the Run cell of the week its date lands in, and in week 1 when no date is set. Then the lane. OUTREACH: the list of the buyer WHO names, in the town the files name (WHO, THE BUYER line of the deck's plan, or a note), one broken thing per row (o1), and the 3 touches, 3 messages to each name (o2); the list enters Wednesday and the touches go out by the founder's hand; week 4 is no new list, and the deck in `squad/mep/` goes in front of the one reply worth an hour, or, with no deck on disk, the deck for that one name (g7). CONTENT: 1 video on what THE SENTENCE sells, the winner in that topic copied, packaged, scripted, cut (c1 to c4), published Wednesday. ADS: 1 concept cut 3 ways, built paused (a2, a3), live in a NEW ad set at the daily number on the founder's go, untouched once it starts. A lesson code gets its plain word the first time it appears. No cell names a day except the send or publish trigger and a client folder's own dated next step |
| Measure | blank when the plan is written. Sunday's 2 numbers, off finished things only: OUTREACH sent and replies (o3); CONTENT 7-day views and asks (c6); ADS cost per lead and leads (a4). Week 1's Sunday reads what went out and little else |
| Improve | blank when the plan is written. Sunday's one line, in the 2 forms beat 4 names. Killing a dead ad and answering people are hygiene, not the change. A change is the brief for Monday's Run, never an edit to what is running |

**Then write `squad/90-day-plan.md` whole**, to `references/plan-template.md`, in its order, and
print ONE message:

- the lane and why, one line off the files, ending "say a different word for a different lane"
- the path, `squad/90-day-plan.md`, then the block line and the 4 Run cells, and nothing more of
  the file

Every number in it is the founder's hours, the arithmetic, or a count a file holds. Never a
projected result.

## 3 · THE YES

**STOP.** Ask for 2 things and nothing more: the yes, and the hour of the block with am or pm
(the plan proposes 9:00 am for the hours a day, Monday to Saturday; the founder confirms or names
another). Another lane word (OUTREACH or CONTENT, and ADS when the `ads gate` row is open), or a
different hours number (the `hours a day` row rewritten with it), rewrites the file, written with
the same care, and prints the same lines. A changed hour reprints the block line only; a yes that
arrives with a new hour is the yes: change the block line, print it, stamp. On the yes: stamp the
header line `confirmed <today's date>`, the only record this gate happened and what the resume
check reads, and write `| daily lane | <lane> |` into `.claude/squad-roots.md`.

Then one line back, opening on the track: OUTREACH, `o1` builds the list this week; CONTENT, `c1`
opens; ADS, `a3` builds the launch, `a2` first when no money card is on disk.

## 4 · THE SUNDAY CELL

The one thing written between the yes and the renewal. The founder says, on a Sunday or after
it:

> Put this in week N's Measure: ____ / Improve: ____

Also "put this in week N" with the 2 parts in any order. Without an N, the week is the row whose
Sunday is today, or the last row that has ended. Then AUTO:

- The Measure cell takes what the founder said: the 2 numbers, or a number and `held until
  <when>` where the thing has not finished. Nothing is computed, nothing is looked up.
- The Improve cell takes exactly 2 forms: one named change, or `holding until <number>, week N`.
  A line in neither form is not written; one line names the 2 forms, and the run waits for the
  line. Never a second question.
- Write the 2 cells of that row in the newest block of `squad/90-day-plan.md`, on disk, print the
  row back, then one line: `/bip sunday` closes on the Improve line; on week 4's Sunday,
  `/the-90-day-plan renew`. Nothing else in the file moves.

A missed Sunday is written, never made up: a row whose Sunday has passed with both cells blank
stays blank until the founder puts a line in it, and the renewal reads the blank as a week nobody
read.

## 5 · RENEW

Week 4's Sunday, or any day after it. A plan is renewed, never extended; 3 plans make the 90
days. **Read first**, asking nothing, and only these:

- the newest block's 4 rows: the Measure and Improve cells. A blank cell is a week nobody read;
  the lane's why line says so.
- `squad/pipeline.md`: replies by bucket, the next touch, what went out last, money received.
- the lane's own log, whichever exists. `squad/outreach-read.md` (o3: the batches, sent and
  replied), `squad/ads-log.md` (a4: one row per week) and the `## Sundays` rows of
  `squad/content-log.md` (c6: views, asks, money, the Improve line) hold the lane's numbers, so
  read every row. The other rows of `squad/content-log.md` are what shipped and when (date,
  title, link, video id); count those for videos published. A lane whose log does not exist prints
  `(no log on disk)`; a missing `squad/pipeline.md` prints `(no pipeline on disk)`. The read runs on
  what is there and the 4 rows.
- beat 2's files again (`.claude/squad-roots.md`, `squad/business.md`,
  `squad/mep/*/plan.md`, `squad/clients/*/notes.md`), and `squad/ads-money-card.md` when the ADS
  read holds, for its daily number.

**Then one message, the only ask in this mode:**

- the hours again: "Hours a day for the system for the next 4 weeks, beyond the hour you spend on
  people?"
- the lane read again off the files as in beat 2, printed with its one line why, and "say a
  different word for a different lane". The founder's word is the lane.

**Then write plan 2 whole**, under `## PLAN 2 of 3 · <today's date>` (PLAN 3 of 3 next). In the
template's order: the header line with the new hours and lane and `confirmed (not yet)`, the
cycle line, the header table, the arithmetic recomputed, the miss clause, and 4 new rows dated
from the Monday after the day the block is written (today, when today is a Monday), the header
line's own rule, numbered 1 to 4 again under their own heading. Week 1's Run carries the last
written Improve line as its brief: a named change becomes what week 1 makes and puts out; a hold
keeps the Run as the lane ran it. Measure and Improve are blank again. Never edit a line above
the new heading. Print the lane line, the path, the block line and the 4 Run cells.

**STOP.** The same gate as beat 3: the yes and the block hour. On the yes, stamp the new header
line `confirmed <date>`, rewrite the `hours a day` and `daily lane` rows in the roots file where
they changed, and print beat 3's line back.

## Rules

- Every message is scannable: a short header, then bullets or a table.
- Never send a message, publish a piece, post anywhere, load a sequence, start an ad set, book a
  slot or spend a budget on their behalf, and never wire anything that would.
- Never price past `squad/business.md`. Nothing here touches a price.
- Never invent a number, a name or a need. Every number printed is one the founder said, a file
  holds, or the arithmetic produced from the hours they said.
- Never sum a post. Money is read off `squad/pipeline.md`, and nowhere else; the lane's numbers
  off its own log and the founder's Sunday line; `squad/posts/` is never opened for numbers.
- Never a menu. One lane, one print, and the founder's word.

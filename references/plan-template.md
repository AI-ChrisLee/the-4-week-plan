# The plan page

`squad/4-week-plan.md`. 1 page, seen not read. This order, nothing else. Plain markdown, a
2-column table where the facts sit side by side, 1-line boxes, no em dashes, no fenced block. It
is a plan, never a promise: nothing in it predicts a result.

These strings are read back by name and written exactly: the `## PLAN` heading, the header line,
the where-you-are line, every `### Week N` heading, and the `Measure:` and `Improve:` lines.

## The block, in order

Every block opens the same way, plan 1 included. `renew` appends the next one below it. Above its
own heading it rewrites only the old block's where-you-are line and takes off its `· this week`
marker.

1. `## PLAN 1 of 3 · <today's date>`. Then `PLAN 2 of 3`, `PLAN 3 of 3`, and after those
   `## PLAN 4 · <date>` and on.
2. The header line:
   `PLAN 1 of 3 · LANE <cold email | YouTube | ads> · hours a day <N> · week 1 <Monday's date> · renew <Monday's date>`

   Week 1 starts the Monday after the block is written (today, when today is a Monday). Renew is
   the Monday after week 4. The block is written at the founder's yes, never before.
3. The cycle line, bold: **Run what already wins. Measure the results. Improve what won.**
4. The where-you-are line, rewritten by every run off today's date, never by hand:
   `WEEK 2 of 4 · Sep 28 to Oct 4 · Sunday Oct 4 writes Measure and Improve`

   Before week 1: `WEEK 1 of 4 · starts <Monday's date>`. After Sunday writes both lines:
   `WEEK 2 of 4 · Sep 28 to Oct 4 · Measure and Improve written`. Past week 4's Sunday:
   `WEEK 4 of 4 ended <date> · say "Renew my plan."`. Once a newer block sits below it, and never
   rewritten again: `WEEK 4 of 4 ended <that week 4's Sunday> · renewed below`.
5. `## Your business`, copied off `squad/business.md` word for word, rewritten only at a renewal.
   A line the page does not carry is left out. No 4th row.

   | Your business | |
   |---|---|
   | The sentence | THE SENTENCE, in bold |
   | Who it is for | WHO, line 1 |
   | The price | PRICE, line 1 |

6. `## The plan`:

   | The plan | |
   |---|---|
   | The lane | `<lane>. <the reason>` |
   | The block | `<start> to <start plus the hours>, Monday to Saturday. Sunday has no block.` The hour the founder gave at the yes |
   | The hour | `1 hour a day on people, by hand. A habit, not a count.` The same in every plan |
   | Sunday | `<the lane's Sunday line>. Only what has finished is read. The rest is held, never guessed.` |

7. `## The weeks`, then 1 line: `A missed week is written, never made up.` Then 4 headings,
   `### Week 1 · Sep 21 to 27`, dated from the header's week 1. The week today falls in ends
   `· this week`. Under each: the boxes, a blank line, `Measure:`, a blank line, `Improve:`. Both
   are empty when the block is written.

## The reason (the lane row)

- ads: `Your money card is on disk, so your ad budget is already set.`
- cold email: `Your buyer is a business with an address on Google Maps, so Agent 4 can pull 50 of them a week.`
- YouTube: `Your buyer is not a business with an address on Google Maps, so your buyer has to find you.`
- the founder's own word: `Your word.`

## The Sunday line (the Sunday row)

- cold email: `"Read my batch." to Agent 4, then "Read my week."`
- YouTube: `"Read my week." It names your video from 8 or more days ago and asks its 7-day views`
- ads: `"Pull my ads read." to Agent 8, then "Read my week."`

## The boxes

Written off the founder's files when the block is written. 1 finishable thing a box: the line to
say in quotes, the agent by number and role. 1 box a week is the send or publish, or 1 box says
nothing goes out that week. Boxes are never counted, totalled or turned into a ratio.

**Every lane, week 1:** no `squad/sales.md` on disk, and the first box is
`- [ ] "/the-close": your sales script, with your booking and payment links (Agent 3, the sales script)`

**Renewal, week 1:** the last written Improve line. A named change is the first box in its own
words, after the Agent 3 box when that one is there. `Same again.`, `run the lane` or a `Hold:`
keeps the lane's boxes. At most 5 boxes: past 5, the change is written into the box it changes.

## The 2 lines Sunday writes

`Measure:` is 1 line, the lane's part first, then the 2 numbers the founder typed. `held` wherever
the thing has not finished.

- cold email: `Measure: batch <date> finished: <n> contacted, <n> replied, <n> bounced, <n> yes · still sending: batch <date>, batch <date> · calls booked <n> · money cleared $<n> (<source> $<n>) · no source $<n>`.
  No batch finished this week: the first part is `held, no batch finished`. No batch still sending:
  that part is left out. No section dated this week in `squad/outreach-read.md`: the first part is
  `held, nothing sent yet` in a week whose boxes carry `Nothing goes out`, else `held, no batch read`.
  Nothing else from the lane.
- YouTube: `Measure: shipped "<title>" · "<title>" 7-day views <n> · calls booked <n> · money cleared $<n> ("<title>" $<n>) · no source $<n>`
- ads: `Measure: from ads <n> calls booked, $<n> a booked call vs $<n> on the card · spent $<n> of the $<n> cap · calls booked <n> · money cleared $<n> (<source> $<n>) · no source $<n>`
  No row whose week ending is this week: the first part is `held, no ads read`.

`Improve:` is the line the founder took, word for word: a named change (Agent 4's `Change:` line,
the change Agent 8's read named, `make 5 more like "<title>"`, or a change in the founder's own
words), `Same again.`, `run the lane`, or a `Hold:`.

## Never in the file

A day count, a done count, a percent, a ratio of boxes, a pace line, a projected result, a
scorecard, a 13th week, a second page.
